# Security & Disclosure Policy

**Last Updated:** November 14, 2026

## 01. Scope & CNA Jurisdiction

This policy applies strictly to assets officially maintained by Linkest Foundation.

- All official open-source repositories (`github.com/Linkest-FDT/*`)
- Official infrastructure and websites (`*.linkest.org`)

In compliance with NIST and MITRE requirements for CVE Numbering Authorities (CNA), we only process reports for the Linkest Foundation ecosystem. We **do not and cannot** process reports for third-party projects. Please report those to their respective maintainers or CNAs.

## 02. Affiliated & Partnered Projects

While the Linkest CNA primarily covers repositories under the `Linkest-FDT` organization, we also extend our CVE assignment and vulnerability triage services to specific Affiliated Projects. These projects are actively supported and maintained by our team but may belong to their own independent organizations or studios.

**Eligibility for CNA Coverage:**
To be recognized as an Affiliated Project eligible for Linkest CNA services, a project must meet **BOTH** of the following criteria simultaneously:

- **Mutual Declaration in README:** The project's official repository must explicitly state its affiliation with Linkest Foundation in its `README.md` (or equivalent documentation).
- **Official Website Listing:** The project must be officially listed on the Linkest Foundation website's "Projects" page.

*Note: Affiliated Projects retain full ownership of their intellectual property. Linkest Foundation does not claim ownership but acts as a trusted security partner providing CNA coverage and triage support.*

## 03. Reporting Channels

- **Primary Channel:** Submit a private vulnerability report (GHSA) directly in our security repository:
  `https://github.com/Linkest-FDT/Security/security/advisories/new`
- **Backup Channel:** If you cannot use GitHub, email us at:
  `security@linkest.org`

*Do not disclose vulnerabilities publicly before a patch is released.*

## 04. Reporting Format

Please format your report (preferably matching the GHSA format) with the following details:

- **Summary:** A brief description of the vulnerability.
- **Details:** Affected repository, version/commit, CWE classification, and root cause.
- **PoC:** Step-by-step reproduction steps or a minimal executable script.
- **Impact:** Affected user scope (e.g., admin takeover).
- **CVSS:** v3.1/v4.0 vector string.

## 05. Triage Priority & SLAs

A reproducible PoC is a critical factor for our prioritization.

- **P0:** CVSS >= 8.0 with PoC. Initial response within 7 days.
- **P1:** CVSS 4.0 - 7.9 or limited impact. Initial response within 14 days.
- **P2:** CVSS < 4.0 or incomplete info. Best effort response.

## 06. CVE Assignment & Disclosure

Once a vulnerability is verified, Linkest CNA will assign an official CVE ID. *(Note: GHSA serves as our collaborative tool for tracking and publishing, while CVE IDs are issued by Linkest CNA).*

We enforce a strict **90-day responsible disclosure** timeline. Public advisories will be published jointly after a patch is available or the 90-day window expires.

## 07. Safe Harbor & Rules of Engagement

- **Safe Harbor Commitment:** We will not take legal action against researchers acting in good faith and following this policy. We will acknowledge your contribution publicly.
- **Prohibited Destructive Attacks:** We strictly reject intentional, volumetric attacks such as large-scale DDoS, brute-forcing, physical damage, or social engineering against staff.
- **Crucial Exception (Vulnerability-Induced DoS):** If you discover a resource exhaustion issue caused **directly by a logic flaw** in our code (e.g., ReDoS, memory leaks triggered by a single request), **this is a valid vulnerability and will be triaged.** However, you must demonstrate it with a controlled, minimal Proof of Concept (PoC). Unbounded stress testing is not allowed.
- **Self-Hosted Testing Principle:** Any potentially disruptive testing (including the logic-based DoS mentioned above) **must be conducted in a local, self-hosted environment**. Do not test against Linkest Foundation's production environment. If live verification is required, you must obtain prior written permission via `security@linkest.org`.
- **Data Boundaries:** You must not access, modify, download, or exfiltrate any real user data not generated by yourself. Any test data created during testing must be deleted immediately.

## 08. Strict Disclaimer

We reserve the right to reject reports for vulnerabilities that are clearly fabricated, maliciously inflated in severity, or submitted with destructive payloads. Attempts to defraud the CVE system will result in public disclosure of the abuse.
